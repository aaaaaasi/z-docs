Console logs kept short ([connect]/[join]/[leave]/[presence]/[disconnect]). SIGTERM/SIGINT graceful shutdown.

Also: /start.sh's bun+mini-services flow auto-runs `bun run dev` for every mini-services/* dir with a package.json on container boot, so the service is covered on restarts too.
